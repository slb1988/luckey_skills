# 风格库参考

从已验证的 prompt 模板中提炼的 8 套核心风格，可作为生成新套装时的参照系或起点。

---

## 1. 深空科技

**核心词**：dark navy, circuit network, geometric nodes, cyan glow  
**情绪**：冷峻、高科技、未来感  
**适用**：AI/技术类博客、科技公司封面  
**构图**：粒子光流从两侧向中心收敛，中心留暗色空间

```
English core
deep space technology landscape, ultra-wide panoramic view, 
dark navy and midnight blue background, glowing cyan and electric blue 
circuit network patterns, floating geometric nodes, subtle particle light streams, 
volumetric depth fog in background, clean negative space in center-left for text overlay,
cinematic horizontal composition, no humans, no text, no logos, 
photorealistic render, 8K quality
```

风格附加词：
- 冷峻极简：minimal, cool tones, soft glow, muted palette
- 炫酷科技：neon accents, holographic shimmer, data visualization aesthetic
- 宇宙星际：nebula depth, cosmic scale, stellar light, deep space atmosphere

---

## 2. 有机渐变

**核心词**：fluid gradient, organic color transitions, noise texture, bokeh  
**情绪**：柔和、现代、少设计感  
**适用**：生活/观点/个人博客  
**构图**：大面积流体色块，左侧自然过渡到暗调

```
English core
fluid gradient abstract background, smooth organic color transitions 
from deep forest green to warm amber to soft coral, subtle noise texture, 
bokeh light scatter, muted earthy palette, large dark low-contrast zone 
on left third for text, no shapes, no patterns, no people, no text, 
soft cinematic grain, 8K render
```

风格附加词：
- 沉静暗调：desaturated, shadow depth, moody atmosphere
- 温暖活力：warm sunrise tones, golden hour glow
- 清冷简约：cool mint and slate, minimal saturation

---

## 3. 建筑极简

**核心词**：concrete texture, geometric shadow lines, monochromatic, side lighting  
**情绪**：高级感、沉稳、克制  
**适用**：商业/深度长文/严肃内容  
**构图**：戏剧性侧光制造长投影线条，左侧深阴影区留文字

```
English core
minimalist architectural interior, ultra-wide 16:9 panoramic,
concrete texture walls, single dramatic side lighting,
long geometric shadow lines, monochromatic palette in charcoal and warm grey, 
shallow depth of field, right side bright window light, 
left side in deep shadow for text placement, 
no furniture, no people, no text,
award-winning architectural photography style, 8K
```

风格附加词：
- 冷峻工业：raw concrete, brutalist, iron grey
- 温润日系：warm wood tone, japanese wabi-sabi, soft diffused light
- 现代奢感：marble surface, polished reflection, luxury minimal

---

## 4. 大气风景

**核心词**：vast minimalist landscape, enormous empty sky, misty, low horizon  
**情绪**：辽阔、呼吸感、孤寂  
**适用**：旅行/人文/随笔/感悟类  
**构图**：地平线压低至底部四分之一，天空占大比例用于文字叠加

```
English core
vast minimalist landscape, ultra-wide 16:9 cinematic,
low horizon line at bottom quarter, enormous empty sky in muted blue-grey, 
single distant mountain silhouette or lone tree, 
misty morning atmosphere, overcast soft light, ultra-low saturation, 
film photography aesthetic, huge negative space in sky area for text overlay,
no people, no text, Hasselblad medium format look, 8K
```

风格附加词：
- 极北旷野：tundra, frozen lake, nordic, silver birch
- 江南烟雨：misty ink wash, east asian minimalism, grey water
- 沙漠孤寂：vast desert dunes, terracotta, dusk gradient

---

## 5. 抽象油画

**核心词**：impasto brushstrokes, oil painting texture, canvas texture, painterly  
**情绪**：艺术感、独特、有手作温度  
**适用**：创意/设计/文化艺术类博客  
**构图**：右侧色块集中，左侧笔触暗区留文字

```
English core
abstract expressionist oil painting texture, ultra-wide 16:9,
thick impasto brushstrokes, deep navy and burnt sienna with gold leaf accents, 
raw canvas texture visible, painterly atmosphere, 
one dominant dark mass on right, large empty brushed-dark area on left for text,
no recognizable objects, no faces, no text,
museum quality fine art aesthetic, macro texture detail, 8K
```

风格附加词：
- 东方水墨：ink wash, sumi-e, rice paper texture, black and indigo
- 克林姆金属：Gustav Klimt inspired, gold foil pattern, jewel tones
- 现代抽象：color field painting, Rothko inspired, soft edge blur

---

## 6. 赛博都市

**核心词**：cityscape silhouette, neon-lit skyline, rain reflection  
**情绪**：夜间感、赛博朋克、霓虹氛围  
**适用**：城市/夜生活/科技炫酷类  
**构图**：远景城市轮廓，近景雨地反光，中景暗色留字

```
English core
cyberpunk cityscape at night, ultra-wide panoramic, 
dark rainy streets with neon reflections, distant skyscraper silhouettes 
glowing in electric purple and magenta, volumetric fog between buildings,
large dark mid-ground zone for text overlay, 
no people, no text, no logos,
cinematic color grading, blade runner aesthetic, 8K photorealistic
```

---

## 7. 自然微距

**核心词**：macro texture, natural material, organic pattern, soft focus  
**情绪**：细腻、宁静、沉浸  
**适用**：美食/生活方式/小众博客  
**构图**：对焦区集中在一侧，另一侧虚焦暗角留文字

```
English core
extreme macro texture photography, ultra-wide 16:9,
organic natural material surface — weathered wood grain or wet stone or 
dried botanical texture, beautiful imperfection and patina, 
right side sharp focus, left side soft bokeh dark gradient for text,
no people, no text, 
Leica macro lens quality, warm neutral tones, 8K detail
```

---

## 8. 极光星空

**核心词**：aurora borealis, starry night, dark landscape silhouette  
**情绪**：壮观、神秘、震撼  
**适用**：科普/宇宙/探索类博客  
**构图**：天空占 70%（极光+星星），地面剪影压底，地平线左右留暗区

```
English core
northern lights aurora borealis over vast dark landscape, ultra-wide 16:9,
vivid green and violet aurora ribbons across a star-filled sky, 
dramatic silhouette of pine trees or mountains at bottom edge,
deep dark sky on sides for text placement, 
no people, no text, no logos,
long exposure night photography, National Geographic quality, 8K
```

## 使用建议

选套装时的快速对照：
- 技术/AI/代码 → 深空科技 或 赛博都市
- 生活/随笔/情感 → 有机渐变 或 大气风景
- 商业/严肃/分析 → 建筑极简
- 创意/艺术/设计 → 抽象油画
- 自然/美食/生活方式 → 自然微距
- 科普/探索/宇宙 → 极光星空
